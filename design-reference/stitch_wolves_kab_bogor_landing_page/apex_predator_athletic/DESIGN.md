## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the aggressive, relentless spirit of high-performance basketball and local community pride in Kabupaten Bogor. Tailored for athletes, passionate fanbases, youth academy prospects, and sports entertainment consumers, the interface projects an unapologetic aura of power, speed, and championship discipline.

Drawing directly from **High-Contrast / Bold** aesthetics married with sharp athletic modernism, the design language mirrors hardwood basketball courts, floodlit night games, and high-octane team spirit. High visual tension is achieved through stark contrasts: obsidian blacks and carbon tones colliding with predatory reds ranging from deep maroon to luminous electric crimson. Typography is commanding and muscular, demanding attention with athletic condensed stances while retaining razor-sharp tactical readability for game stats, rosters, ticketing, and live streaming.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an authentic basketball franchise identity: nocturnal, lethal, and fiery.

- **Primary (`#ff0000` / Electric Red):** High-voltage accent for dynamic calls-to-action, active game states, live broadcast indicators, and critical player statistics. Used deliberately to create fiery focal points against dark backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#bc0202` / Crimson Red):** The team's signature heart-rate color. Powers secondary actions, badges, score differentials, active navigation outlines, and interactive hover gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#830000` / Blood Oxblood):** Deep foundational red used for subtle structural surfaces, dark gradients, container edge highlights, and high-impact sports jersey motif backdrops.
- **Neutral Surface & Dark Ground (`#121212` / Carbon Neutral, stepping down to `#000000` Pitch Black):** The primary canvas. True black anchors heroes and stadium imagery, while `#121212` and `#1a1a1a` shape card containers, tables, and modal backgrounds.
- **Accent Light (`#ffffff`):** Pure optical white reserved for headlines, primary label typography, and high-visibility jersey numbers against dark fills.

## Typography

The type scale channels the adrenaline of arena courtside displays and dynamic broadcast packages.

- **Display & Headlines (`Anybody`):** Set in ultra-bold and black weights with tight tracking and uppercase treatments for match fixtures, squad names, and club proclamations. Its muscular, variable-width geometry gives instant kinetic sports presence.
- **Body & Data (`Space Grotesk`):** Highly legible, engineered sans-serif providing distinct technical precision. It guarantees instant reading speed for game logs, box scores, play-by-play timelines, and tournament schedules without fatigue.
- **Labels & Microcopy (`Anybody`):** Formatted in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic arena boards, player tags, and athletic jersey lettering.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column fluid grid governs desktop viewports, condensing to an 8-column layout on tablets and a tight 4-column layout on mobile devices.

Layout spaces are tight and assertive, reflecting the density of court play and fast breaks. Wide desktop canvas margins (`3rem`) narrow down to `1.25rem` on mobile screens to preserve maximum visual impact for stats, schedules, and photography. Component padding follows multiples of `0.25rem` (4px baseline), pairing dense stat tables (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) with expansive hero and roster feature cards (`space-lg` and `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating cloud-like shadows in favor of aggressive sports-grade depth:

- **Tonal Layering:** The foundational floor sits at `#000000`. Mid-level surfaces (cards, standings tables, player tiles) use `#121212`. Elevated overlays (drawers, flyouts, active squad detail sheets) rise to `#1a1a1a`.
- **Neon Crimson Back-Glows:** Key interactive and live components project subtle, directional ambient halos (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 0, 0, 0.28)`), communicating high energy without washing out the dark mode fidelity.
- **Crisp Structural Borders:** Surfaces are delineated by razor-thin dark crimson or carbon-subtle borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `1px solid #830000`), referencing court line-markings and technical stadium architecture.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a decisive, aerodynamic, low-radius posture (`roundedness: 1`). 

Elements employ clean, athletic corners (typically `4px` on buttons and chips, scaling to `8px` on match cards and roster containers). This maintains an aggressive, predatory silhouette reminiscent of claw marks, court boundaries, and team emblems, rejecting overly bubbly or friendly pill shapes in favor of sharp athletic power.

## Components

- **Buttons:** 
  - *Primary Match CTA:* Solid `#ff0000` with pure white uppercase `Anybody` text, 4px border radius, sharp hover state shifting to `#bc0202` with an inner top rim highlight (`rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`).
  - *Secondary Ghost CTA:* Dark `#121212` background framed by a `1.5px solid #bc0202` border, brightening text to `#ff0000` on hover.
  - *All buttons:* Angular, uppercase typography with tracking and compact padding (`0.75rem 1.5rem`).
- **Chips & Tags:** 
  - Slender rectangular tags (`border-radius: 2px`) featuring game status tags (`LIVE`, `FINAL`, `Q3`).
  - Active/Live chips pulsate with `#ff0000` background and white text; inactive tags employ `#1a1a1a` fill with `#830000` edge strokes.
- **Match & Roster Cards:**
  - Dark carbon background (`#121212`) framed with a subtle `1px solid rgba(131, 0, 0, 0.4)` border.
  - Hover transitions feature an aggressive left-accent stroke in `#ff0000` (`3px solid #ff0000`) and a subtle red-ambient perimeter glow.
- **Input Fields & Search:**
  - Charcoal canvas (`#161616`) with high-contrast white text, placeholder in muted grey (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), and an active focus outline of `#ff0000` with zero blur for crisp, decisive input.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Square form factor with beveled corners, dark `#1a1a1a` base, switching on activation to solid `#ff0000` with an uncompromising white glyph check.
- **Scoreboard & Stat Strips:**
  - Purpose-built athletic component utilizing the `label-stat` token. High contrast score blocks featuring home versus away panels, split by deep oxblood dividers (`#830000`).
